Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Keyboard Cleaner ("the App") is a macOS utility that temporarily blocks keyboard input so you can clean your keyboard. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how. The short version: the App does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party software development kits that collect data. The App runs inside Apple's App Sandbox without permission to make network connections, so it cannot send information off your Mac.

Accessibility Permission

To block every key, including function, media, and system shortcut keys, the App asks you to allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. The App uses this permission for one purpose only: while a cleaning session is active, it intercepts keyboard events (and, if you turn on the option, trackpad and mouse clicks) and discards them before they reach macOS or any other app.

  • The App never records, logs, saves, or transmits what you type.
  • The App does not read the content of other apps, windows, or documents.
  • To light up the on-screen keyboard map, the App notes which key positions were pressed during the current session. This list lives only in your Mac's memory, is never written to disk, and is cleared when the next session starts or the App quits.
  • When a session ends, the App stops intercepting input entirely.

You can revoke this permission at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. The App will keep working in a limited mode that only catches keys sent to the App itself.

Information Stored on Your Mac

The App saves your preferences, such as the auto-unlock timer, whether trackpad clicks are locked, sound settings, and whether the menu bar icon is shown, using the standard macOS preferences system. These preferences stay on your Mac inside the App's sandbox container, and we have no access to them. Deleting the App removes them.

Information Shared With Third Parties

We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any. If you download the App from the Mac App Store, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy, such as purchase and download records. That information is handled by Apple, not by us.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal information about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, please contact us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page and include the updated policy in the App. Material changes will be noted in the App's release notes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support link on the App's Mac App Store page.
